AI analysis is advisory only.

Every entity, threat flag, and case link must be treated as a lead
until reviewed by a human investigator.

Rules:
1. Never automatically confirm an AI result.
2. Always expose confidence.
3. Threat snippets must be exact substrings of the evidence.
4. Entity values must occur in the evidence.
5. Ambiguous results should remain pending.
6. Self-harm risk requires human review.
7. Suggested case links require human confirmation.
8. Never invent evidence.
9. Return structured JSON when requested.
